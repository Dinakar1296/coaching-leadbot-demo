Monday 27 July · Warwick campus → Birmingham → back

Five hours in Birmingham, after everything has shut

You land at 19:00. Every museum is already closed. That's fine — the thing worth seeing in Birmingham at night is the water, and it's a four-minute walk from the station.

01The call

Go
BirminghamTrains run five an hour and the last one back leaves at 00:41. It is the only option in range that survives a 19:00 start.
Skip
Stratford-upon-AvonLast direct train out is ~19:27 — you'd arrive as it leaves. Shakespeare's Birthplace, Holy Trinity, Anne Hathaway's Cottage all close at 17:00. Go on a Saturday morning instead.
Already in
The canalsYour friends are right, but the canals are Birmingham — Gas Street Basin is in the city centre. Don't treat it as a separate trip, and don't give it ten minutes. It's the main event.

02Getting there

17:50

University Interchange → Coventry

Bus 12X (direct) or 11 to Coventry city centre. Roughly 20–25 min. Don't aim for Canley station — only about six trains a day stop there.

18:25

Walk to Coventry station

About 10 minutes from the Bull Yard / Pool Meadow area. Buy an off-peak day return to Birmingham, not two singles — after 18:30 you're in off-peak territory anyway.

18:40

Coventry → Birmingham New Street

20–25 minutes, direct, five trains an hour. No need to plan a specific one — turn up and take the next.

19:05

Out at New Street

Leave via Grand Central and exit onto Stephenson Street. Sunset is 21:14 — you have two hours of daylight, so spend them outside and save the interiors for after dark.

03The evening

19:05

Bullring, Selfridges, St Martin's

Two minutes from the station and it closes at 20:00 on a Monday, so do it first or not at all. The Selfridges disc façade and the old church sitting underneath it is the one postcard shot in Birmingham that isn't the canals.

19:35

Victoria Square → Chamberlain Square → Paradise

Ten minutes uphill on foot. Council House, the "Floozie in the Jacuzzi" fountain, then through the new Paradise development into Centenary Square with its ground fountains. Free, outdoors, all open.

20:00

Library of Birmingham

The gold filigree building on Centenary Square. Open until 20:00 Mon–Thu, so this is a walk-in-and-look, not a sit-down — worth the detour for the atrium alone. The rooftop Secret Garden closes at 15:00, so don't climb up expecting it.

20:20

Gas Street Basin and Brindleyplace — the main event

Drop down from Broad Street to the water. Do the loop properly: Gas Street Basin → the 1827 cast-iron footbridge → Brindleyplace → under Black Sabbath Bridge → back along to the Mailbox. Give it a full 60–90 minutes. You'll catch it in daylight, at sunset, and lit up — which is the whole reason to be here tonight.

21:30

Dinner, canalside or Chinatown

Two easy calls. Brindleyplace / the Mailbox puts you at a table over the water without moving. The Arcadian & Chinatown (8 min from the Bullring, near the Hippodrome) is livelier and open later on a quiet Monday.

22:30

One last thing, if you still have legs

Rooftop drink on the 25th floor of The Cube at Wharfside Street — 360° over the city, and it's already on your canal loop. Or if you'd rather do something than look at something, Golf Fang in Digbeth runs Mondays 15:00–22:00, last activity 21:00 — book ahead and slot it earlier in the night.

23:00

Walk back to New Street

Everything above is inside a 15-minute walk of the station. Aim to be on a train by 23:15.

04Getting back — read this one

Last train · 00:41

The last Birmingham New Street → Coventry train leaves at 00:41, and there are five an hour before that. So midnight is comfortable, not tight — but the bus from Coventry back to campus stops well before the train does, and late July is the summer timetable.

Plan for a taxi at the Coventry end. Coventry station → campus is a 10-minute ride, roughly £8–12 on Uber or Bolt, split between you. Leave Birmingham on the 23:15-ish train and you're on campus by about 00:00.

Don't gamble on catching the last 12X. Check its final departure on the National Express app before you leave campus tonight — if it's gone, the taxi is the plan, not the emergency.

06Before you leave campus

  • Check the last 12X back from Coventry to University Interchange tonight — then assume you'll miss it.
  • Buy off-peak day returns at Coventry, or on the app, before boarding.
  • Have Uber or Bolt installed with a payment card that works in the UK.
  • Glance at the Birmingham Hippodrome listings — a show runs from tonight through 7 August, and a 19:30 curtain would replace the whole itinerary above.
  • Take a light jacket. It's a canal at 22:00.
