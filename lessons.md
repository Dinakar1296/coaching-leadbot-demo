# Lessons

Rules written after corrections, so the same mistakes don't happen again.

1. **Never list a book, paper or title unless it has been verified.** A made-up title ("The Boxing Bible" by Andy Dumas and Jeremy Laxton) and a wrong title (Hatmaker) slipped into the boxing guide. If a title can't be verified, leave it out.
2. **Plan timelines must respect the processing times stated in the same document.** The company plan expected a certificate of incorporation 2 days after filing while the guide itself said 7 to 10 working days. Schedule government steps with buffers, and put dependent steps after the documents they depend on.
3. **Check safety advice against current official guidance, not memory.** The "damp cloth on an oil fire" advice has been withdrawn by fire services. For any safety step (fire, food, first aid, sport), cite a current official source.
4. **Never schedule a paid, regulated or risky activity before the registration, insurance and certification it needs.** The exploration plan had a paid trek and a drone flight before registration, insurance and a pilot certificate.
5. **"Reimbursement," "grant" and "subsidy" are not the same.** Read the repayment terms before describing any government money.
6. **For any "current as of" guide, search specifically for changes in the last 12 months** (rules, deadlines, scheme closures), not just for the rule itself.
7. **Budget web searches.** Subagents share the session's search limit (200). Spend searches on facts that change (laws, fees, dates), and write stable-science content from references.
8. **Test code on more than one platform.** Port 5000 is taken on modern Macs, and `od -t x1z` is Linux-only.
