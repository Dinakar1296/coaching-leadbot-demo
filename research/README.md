# 30-Day Plans: Deep Research Pack

Five separate guides. Each has **Part 1: everything you need to know**, **Part 2: a day-by-day 30-day plan**, **Part 3: resources**, and a **sources** list. The India-specific rules were researched in **September 2026**.

| # | Guide | What it covers | Daily time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Cooking from Zero](01-cooking.md) | Kitchen setup, food safety (FSSAI/USDA temperatures), knife skills, heat science, every core method, Indian + global techniques, flavour balance, baking, nutrition (ICMR-NIN 2024), meal prep | 1 to 1.5 h |
| 2 | [Entry-Level Boxing](02-boxing.md) | Gear, hand wrapping, stance, footwork, all 6 punches, defence, combinations, conditioning, safety and concussion, rules, training in India (BFI) | 45 to 75 min, 6 days/week |
| 3 | [Talking to Computers in Binary](03-binary-computing.md) | Number systems, two's complement, floats, ASCII/UTF-8, logic gates, CPU, machine code, assembly, OS, UART/I2C/SPI, error detection, TCP/IP, sockets, your own protocol, Arduino project | 1 to 1.5 h |
| 4 | [Starting a Company in India](04-starting-a-company.md) | Idea validation, legal structures, SPICe+ registration, post-incorporation and annual compliance, Income-tax Act 2025, GST 2.0, DPIIT 2026 startup rules, MSME, funding schemes, labour codes, DPDP | 2 to 3 h |
| 5 | [Starting an Exploration Company in India](05-exploration-company.md) | Every kind of exploration: minerals (Exploration Licences, NMEDT, NCMM), offshore and deep-sea, oil and gas (OALP), space (IN-SPACe), adventure and expeditions (IMF, permits), polar (Antarctic Act), biodiversity, archaeology (ASI), drones and geospatial | About 3 h |

## Running all five at the same time

Doing all five at full depth takes about **8 to 10 hours a day**. That is realistic only if this is your full-time focus. Two options:

**Option A: Everything in parallel (full-time, about 8 h/day)**

| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| Morning (1 h) | Boxing session |
| Late morning (2.5 h) | Company guide (Guide 4) |
| Lunch (1 h) | Cooking day's recipe (you eat what you cook) |
| Afternoon (2.5 h) | Exploration guide (Guide 5) |
| Evening (1 h) | Binary guide (Guide 3) |

Guides 4 and 5 overlap in Week 3 (both register a company). If you're building an exploration company, **register only one company**: follow the Guide 4 legal steps, using the Guide 5 structure advice.

**Option B: Staggered (part-time, about 3 to 4 h/day)**
- **Month 1:** Cooking + Boxing + Binary (physical and learning skills, about 3.5 h/day)
- **Month 2:** Starting a company + Exploration company (business, about 4 h/day). These two use the same legal and funding steps

## Important notes
- **Laws and fees change.** Every legal or financial number cites its source. Confirm it on the official portal before filing or paying.
- **Not professional advice.** Use a Chartered Accountant, Company Secretary or lawyer for filings and contracts.
- **Boxing:** no sparring during the 30 days. See a doctor first if you have health conditions.
- **Exploration:** never carry satellite phones or satellite messengers in India without DoT permission, and never enter restricted areas without permits.
