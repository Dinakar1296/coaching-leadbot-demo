## How to schedule this (all 15 at once is not realistic)

The first five together need about **8 to 10 hours a day**. Adding the other ten isn't possible in parallel. Choose one of these:

**Option A: Three months, staggered (about 3 to 5 hours a day)**

| Month | Physical / practical | Learning | Business |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Month 1** | Cooking (1 to 1.5 h) + Boxing (1 h) | Human Body (1 h) + Ancient Books reading (30 to 45 min) | None |
| **Month 2** | Martial Arts sampler (1 h) | Binary (1 to 1.5 h) + Sound (45 min) + Void or Higher Dimensions (45 min) | None |
| **Month 3** | Keep cooking and training | Life (45 min) + Planets (45 min) | Company (2 to 3 h), or Exploration (3 h) if that's your company |
| **Month 4 (optional)** | | Ancient Technology + Upper and Lower Realms (1.5 h) | Whichever business guide you didn't do |

**Option B: One focus at a time** (the highest success rate). Do one guide per month, in the order that matters most to you. That takes 15 months, and you'll finish far more of them.

**Guides that pair well:**
- Boxing + Martial Arts + Human Body (training and the body)
- Binary + Sound (digital audio is binary)
- Void + Higher Dimensions + Planets (physics and cosmology)
- Ancient Books + Upper and Lower Realms + Life (traditions)
- Company + Exploration (the same legal steps: register only one company)

## Important notes
- **Laws and fees change.** Confirm every legal or financial number on the official portal before filing or paying.
- **Not professional advice.** Use a Chartered Accountant, Company Secretary, lawyer or doctor where relevant.
- **Physical safety:** no sparring in the boxing plan; learn throws and chokes only on mats with a coach; see a doctor first if you have health conditions.
- **Exploration:** never carry satellite phones or satellite messengers in India without DoT permission; never enter restricted areas without permits.
- **Belief topics** (07, 09, 10, 13, 14, 15) keep evidence, tradition and unproven claims clearly separate, and describe every tradition respectfully.
