💷 Money Plan

Main spend money: ₹30,000 loaded on the Niyo card arranged through college (roughly £260–280).

Cash plan confirmed: Take only ~£50 in cash — UK is almost fully cashless. Card/phone tap works everywhere: buses, trains, shops, even street food stalls. Withdraw more from an ATM there only if genuinely needed.
ATM tip: Use only ATMs marked "FREE cash withdrawals" (bank ATMs, supermarket ATMs). Avoid Euronet / travel-money ATMs — they charge £2–3 per withdrawal and offer a bad rate ("decline conversion, pay in GBP" if asked). Check the Niyo app for its own per-withdrawal fee.

🔌 Electronics

UK uses Type G (big 3-pin) sockets — Indian plugs do NOT fit. Voltage is the same 230V, so no converter needed, only adapters.

👕 Clothes & Shoes (7 days)

🧴 Toiletries, Medicines & Food

UK customs: No meat or dairy products allowed in from India (this includes ghee sweets, paneer items). Sealed dry vegetarian snacks are fine.
